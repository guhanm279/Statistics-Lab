Binomial Distribution

Calculates exact moments, variance, skewness, and probability mass for $n$ trials with success rate $p$.

$$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$$

Analytical Verification ($n=20, p=0.35$)
• Mean ($\mu$): $20 \times 0.35 = \mathbf{7.0000}$
• Variance ($\sigma^2$): $20 \times 0.35 \times 0.65 = \mathbf{4.5500}$
• 2nd Moment ($\mathbb{E}[X^2]$): $4.55 + 7^2 = \mathbf{53.5500}$
• Skewness ($\gamma_1$): $(1 - 2(0.35)) / \sqrt{4.55} = 0.30 / 2.1331 = \mathbf{0.1406}$

$P(X = k)$ for $0 \le k \le n$

Mathematical Formula Reference

Mean: $\mu = np$
Variance: $\sigma^2 = np(1-p)$
2nd Moment: $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = \text{Var}(X) + \mathbb{E}[X]^2$
Skewness: $\gamma_1 = \frac{1-2p}{\sqrt{np(1-p)}}$
