Binomial Distribution

Models the number of successes in $n$ independent trials with success probability $p$.

$$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$$

Binomial Distribution Module

Configure trial parameters $n$ and $p$ with live validation and theoretical statistics

$X \sim \text{Bin}(n, p)$

Input Parameters

Must be positive integer ($n \ge 1$)
Invalid $n$: Must be a positive integer ($n \ge 1$).
Range: $0 \le p \le 1$
Invalid $p$: Probability must be between $0.0$ and $1.0$.
Validation Rules
  • $n$ represents discrete Bernoulli trial occurrences.
  • $p$ represents single-trial outcome success rate.

Selected Values & Theoretical Summary

Valid Parameters
Trials ($n$) 20
Success Prob ($p$) 0.35
Failure Prob ($q = 1-p$) 0.65
Expected Mean ($\mathbb{E}[X] = n \cdot p$) 7.000
Variance ($\text{Var}(X) = npq$) 4.550
Std Deviation ($\sigma = \sqrt{npq}$) 2.133
Formulas & Definitions
• Probability Mass: $P(X=k) = \binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$
• Expected Value: $\mathbb{E}[X] = n \cdot p = 20 \times 0.35 = 7.0$
• Variance: $\text{Var}(X) = n \cdot p \cdot (1-p) = 4.55$
• Standard Dev: $\sigma = \sqrt{\text{Var}(X)} = 2.133$
